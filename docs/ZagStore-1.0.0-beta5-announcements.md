## Download section

**Your Apple IDs. Your certificates. Your freedom.**

**One store. Multiple Apple IDs. Your own certificates.**

Download Zag Store 1.0.0 Beta 5 (Build 5) for iOS and iPadOS 16.4 or later. Set up the signing method that works for you once Zag Store is installed.

- **Apple ID signing.** Connect one or more free Apple IDs, choose the active account for new signing jobs, and sign eligible apps with your Personal Team. Apple’s Personal Team limits apply.
- **Certificate signing.** Bring your supported signing certificate and provisioning profile and use the certificate workflow you already know.

**Download Zag Store Beta 5**

## Social

**Your Apple IDs. Your certificates. Your freedom.**

Zag Store 1.0.0 Beta 5 brings native free Apple ID signing to iPhone and iPad. Keep multiple Apple IDs connected, choose one for new installs, and keep using your own signing certificates. Refresh All handles eligible apps across both methods.

## In-app What’s New

**Your Apple IDs. Your certificates. Your freedom.**

Sign eligible apps with a free Apple ID, keep multiple accounts connected, or use your own signing certificates. Pick the active account for new installs in **Apple IDs**. Use **Refresh All** to renew eligible apps with the account or certificate that originally signed them.
